## 2026 Search, AEO, GEO and LLMO principles

Apply current official Search guidance:

- Traditional SEO remains the foundation for AI Overviews and AI Mode.
- No special AI schema is required.
- Do not rely on `llms.txt`, artificial content chunking or AI-only pages.
- Keep important content in crawlable HTML.
- Use strong internal links.
- Provide original, non-commodity information.
- Use high-quality images/video where they add value.
- Maintain page experience and technical accessibility.
- Ensure structured data matches visible content.
- Follow Search spam policies in AI results as well as normal Search.
- Avoid scaled content abuse, doorway pages, fake mentions, fabricated proof and deceptive claims.

AEO implementation:

- answer the question directly under relevant headings;
- use comparison tables, checklists and process explanations;
- answer procurement and technical follow-up questions;
- link from answer pages to service, case-study and RFQ pages.

GEO/LLMO implementation:

- maintain consistent company/service/location/project entities;
- publish verifiable original facts and evidence;
- cite official sources and document dates for standards content;
- make the distinction between verified fact and project-specific assumption;
- earn legitimate third-party mentions through real work, not manufactured placements.
